Open-source projects

Each one is tested and type-checked (mypy strict) in CI. The result beside it is something I measured.

  • docfunnel

    pypdfium2, RapidOCR, Pydantic, FastAPI, MCP

    Turns PDFs and scans into data an LLM can be trusted with: fast text extraction, OCR only on the pages that need it, schema-validated extraction with retries and model fallback, and evals that track accuracy, invented fields, cost and latency. Usable from a CLI, an API or as an MCP tool.

    Sends a page to OCR only when needed, right 99.3% of the time, and reads light scans with a 1.6% character error rate.

  • lgpd-face-redactor

    OpenCV (YuNet, SFace), FastAPI, Docker

    Blurs every face in a video except the people you choose, for LGPD and GDPR access requests. Upload the footage, tick who stays visible, then download the redacted copy and an audit record. Runs on your own machine.

    99.38% verification accuracy on LFW, and a policy that blurs whenever a match is in doubt.

  • dental-analytics-medallion

    dbt, DuckDB, OpenAI structured outputs, Streamlit

    A bronze, silver and gold pipeline over California's public dental data, with a dashboard and an LLM brief whose every figure is checked against the warehouse before anyone reads it.

    The audit found services counted twice: 71.2 million became 42.6 million, and 505 duplicate rows came out.

  • marketing-report-generator

    pandas, statsmodels, SciPy

    Funnel and channel analysis of messy CRM exports, reporting intervals and corrected significance tests instead of bare percentages.

    Checked against known true rates on 200 simulated exports per size: it flags the better channel in 89% of them at 5,000 prospects, and in the wrong direction at most 1%.

  • uerj-marica-transit-network

    NetworkX, MapLibre

    A graph model of the public bus network of Maricá, RJ: centrality, shortest routes, reach by number of transfers and stop spacing, with an interactive explorer in Portuguese and English.

    614 stops and 46 lines. Removed a fake hub created when 33 stops were geocoded to one point.

  • uerj-optimization-methods

    NumPy, SciPy, Pyodide

    Nelder–Mead written from its definition, and a constrained tank-design optimizer using Newton and quasi-Newton methods, both runnable in a browser lab.

    Matches SciPy's evaluation count on every benchmark. The original solver reported an infeasible tank as the optimum; now it doesn't.

More coursework, rewritten as tested packages: finite-difference and TVD advection schemes, search, constraint satisfaction and an MLP from scratch, and least squares with inference and diagnostics.
